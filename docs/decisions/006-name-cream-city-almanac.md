# 006: Name: Cream City Almanac

**Decision:** The product is called **Cream City Almanac**, credited as "Built on Data You Can Use's public data."

**Why this came up:** The product is unofficial, so it can't use DYCU's name as its own. The comps needed a real name, and the name shapes how reporters and portfolio reviewers read the product in the first second.

**Options:**
1. **Bubbler:** Milwaukee's word for a drinking fountain ("data on tap"). Cost: outside Wisconsin it's also slang for a cannabis pipe, and a Madison library program already uses "The Bubbler".
2. **Cream City Almanac:** Milwaukee's nickname (from its cream-colored brick) plus "almanac", a reference of facts organized by year. Cost: long, and less punchy.
3. **Field Guide MKE** or **Tract:** describe the job or the data's unit. Cost: generic phrase, or insider jargon.

**What we chose and why:** Cream City Almanac (Tarik's choice; Claude had recommended Bubbler). It's clear to outsiders, local to insiders, and "almanac" matches the product's year-by-year organization. The design deliberately avoids the old-almanac look the name invites (see 005).

**What we gave up:** A shorter, punchier name.

**How we'll know if this was right:** People outside Milwaukee can say what the product is from its name plus tagline, and nobody confuses it for an official DYCU product.

Checks on 2026-10-07: no existing product by this name found in a web search; `creamcityalmanac.com` and `.org` were available (not registered); no formal trademark search done.
