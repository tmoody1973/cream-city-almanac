# 018: A Start here page, and DYCU's guide pages are not data

**Decision:** Add a Start here page with three worked examples (a reporter, a nonprofit, a resident) that show live excerpts of the real data, followed by DYCU's own guide pages. Those guide pages (Getting Started, About the Data, Questions and Feedback) leave the "Updated this season" list and open as a short "guide page on DYCU's Hub" block instead of a dataset sheet.

**Why this came up:** How it works explains how the almanac is built, but nothing shows a newcomer what they would do with it. Meanwhile DYCU's three guide pages were filed among the datasets: two of them took slots in "Updated this season", and their sheets were nearly empty (the AI explainer for Getting Started says its description is "an unfilled template placeholder"). Both problems are about orientation, so one page answers both.

**Options:**
1. **One Start here page** with the examples and the guides, and guide pages out of the data list. Cost: a new page with its own design round; a small change to the rundown and the sheet.
2. **Examples on their own page, guides linked from How it works and the footer.** Cost: orientation lives in two places.
3. **Leave the guide pages where they are.** Cost: they keep crowding the data list and opening empty sheets.

For the examples' data: live excerpts pulled from the data; words and links only; or numbers typed into the text with a date.

**What we chose and why:** Option 1 with live excerpts (Tarik, on Claude's recommendations). Live excerpts are the only way to show real numbers that stay true when DYCU's data refreshes each week, and they follow the product rule that numbers come from the data, never from someone's typing. The three people match who the tool is for: reporters, nonprofits writing grants, and residents.

**What we gave up:** A simpler page. Live excerpts need their own query and design work, and the examples depend on search ranking: if "older housing and asthma rates" stops finding Housing Built Before 1950, the reporter example has to change.

**How we'll know if this was right:** First-time visitors who open Start here go on to open a sheet; the guide pages stop showing up as "updated" data; nobody reports an example whose numbers don't match the app.
