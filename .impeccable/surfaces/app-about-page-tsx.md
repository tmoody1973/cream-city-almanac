# /about: What's in the Almanac

**Mode:** Persuade. **Route:** `/about` (app/about/page.tsx). Linked from the footer, Start here and the masthead menu.

**Audience:** a Milwaukee resident who has never heard of the Almanac (often sent a link), plus reporters and nonprofit staff deciding whether to rely on it.

**Job:** understand what the Almanac is and what it can do in one scroll, then look up their own address.

**Primary action:** the live address box (entry 1). Secondary: open Ask, open Start here.

**Proof:** live real parts only: the address box itself, a real fact-sheet row (Riverwest poverty vs the city, from stored data), a live City count card (Harambee robberies this year), the real source tags. No screenshots, no invented numbers, no testimonials.

**Copy source:** docs/marketing/cream-city-almanac-one-pager.md (humanized, fact-checked against the live site, 2026-10-16). Do not add claims beyond it.

**Constraints:** phone first, with its own laptop composition (not a stretched phone column); prints as a one-page handout; WCAG 2.2 AA; unofficial, credit line to DYCU.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the almanac's own front matter: a numbered table of contents in which every entry is a working part, starting with your address. It refuses the marketing-hero-plus-feature-grid arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: White paper, black ink, 2px rules opening each entry, gray bands for data rows, giant condensed numerals 1–7 in the left gutter, condensed capitals for entry titles, red grease pencil only as one underline and one handwritten margin note.

STORY: The visitor learns the Almanac answers Milwaukee questions with sourced numbers, sees it work on real data, believes it because every number shows who wrote it, and types their address.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead; WHAT'S IN THE ALMANAC across the full width; one body line; entry 1 YOUR ADDRESS with the full-width address field and FIND button; entry 2's Riverwest row beginning at the fold. Primary action is entry 1.

FORM: The Almanac's Contents, position 4 of 7 on the ranked list, seed key 5767e6d8.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
