# Surface brief: How it works (/how-it-works), phone and laptop

- **Scope / mode:** A plain-English page explaining the product and how to read a dataset. Mode: Persuade (portfolio link) and teach (first-time reporter).
- **Audience and job:** A first-time visitor understands in under a minute what this is, where the data comes from, what the AI does and doesn't do, and how to start.
- **Proof / content:** Live counts from the catalog (omitted, never invented, when it can't be reached); a real W01 sheet; DYCU's own column definitions verbatim. No testimonials, usage numbers or DYCU endorsement.
- **Constraints:** WCAG 2.2 AA; renders without JavaScript; red only as grease pencil (here also the hand-written teaching notes), never numbered.
- **Approved comps:** laptop `.impeccable/mocks/how-laptop.webp`, phone `.impeccable/mocks/how-phone.webp` (notes above each part, arrows down).
