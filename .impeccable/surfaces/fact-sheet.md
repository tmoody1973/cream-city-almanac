# Fact sheet (/n/[slug])

**Scope:** one printable page per DYCU profiled area (27): ~9 headline figures beside the City of Milwaukee's, verdicts, map, folded tables, sources, print/export. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-neighborhood-fact-sheet-design.md.

**Audience:** nonprofit staff writing grants and preparing meetings; reporters; residents. Read on a laptop, printed to one US Letter page, opened on a phone from a link.

**Approved comps (Tarik, 2026-10-12): direction A, "the ledger"**
- laptop: .impeccable/mocks/fact-sheet-a-laptop.webp (primary)
- phone: .impeccable/mocks/fact-sheet-a-phone.webp
- print: .impeccable/mocks/fact-sheet-a-print.webp

**Direction contract**
- THESIS: a newspaper data ledger. One ruled table, FIGURE | {NAME} | CITY OF MILWAUKEE | COMPARED; the big population numeral above it; map + notes + sources below.
- OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md's Marked-Up Rundown. Paper, ink, grey band row stripes, Saira uppercase labels, hairline rules, no cards/shadows/radii.
- Red is only grease pencil: a hand-drawn circle around each "clearly higher/lower" verdict (higher: circle; lower: underline). Verdict words are printed type written by code, never handwriting.
- Phone: each row stacks (label; name figure; city figure; verdict) — four squeezed columns are not allowed.
- Print: no nav, buttons or folded tables; one Letter page.
- Not to literalize: the comp's stray "year switcher" text; the comp's map drawing (the real map is the site's MapLibre tract map); printed nav in the print comp.
